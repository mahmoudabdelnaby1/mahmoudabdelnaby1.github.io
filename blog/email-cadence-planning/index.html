PRACTICAL GUIDE · Email marketing

Set Email Cadence Around Value and Expectations

Choose a rhythm the audience understands and the team can sustain.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Increasing volume to fill a calendar can reduce relevance and raise fatigue.

Practical workflow

  1. State signup expectations.
  2. Inventory useful updates and timing.
  3. Set maximum frequency and suppression.
  4. Review engagement and complaints before adjusting.

Working template

Expected cadence: _ / value per send: / max frequency: _ / suppressions: / review date: ____.

Applied example

A monthly digest can fit a low-change service better than weekly emails repeating unchanged information.

Measure and review

Compare useful actions with complaints and unsubscribes by cohort.

Guardrail

Do not raise frequency only to chase opens.
