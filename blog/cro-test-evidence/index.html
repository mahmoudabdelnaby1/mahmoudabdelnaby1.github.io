Decide Whether an A/B Test Has Enough Evidence

Choose a hypothesis, primary measure and stopping rule before opening a test.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Short tests can turn random fluctuation into a confident but unreliable story.

A practical workflow

  1. Write one falsifiable hypothesis.
  2. Select a measure close to the business outcome.
  3. Estimate baseline and available traffic.
  4. Avoid mid-test changes; label inconclusive results honestly.

Applied example

If only a handful of visitors complete each variant, usability interviews may identify confusion faster than calling a noisy conversion result a winner.

Measure and review

If traffic is insufficient, use usability observation and label its evidence type.

Guardrail

Do not declare a winner from a small sample or after repeated unplanned peeking.
