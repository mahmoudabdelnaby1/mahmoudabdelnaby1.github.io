Build a Social Calendar Around Audience Questions

Give every post a job grounded in a real audience question.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A calendar can be full yet disconnected. Begin with recurring questions and match each to a useful format.

A practical workflow

  1. Collect questions from conversations and site searches.
  2. Group by awareness and decision stage.
  3. Choose a format and source for each answer.
  4. Assign owner, response route and a slot for timely topics.

Working template

Question: _ / stage: / answer: _ / format: / source: _ / next action: _ / owner: ____.

Applied example

Repeated questions about service coverage can become a short FAQ post, a saved response for the community team, and a detailed destination page.

Measure and review

Review meaningful replies, saves, qualified visits and next-step completions by format.

Guardrail

Reach alone does not prove the calendar serves its audience.
