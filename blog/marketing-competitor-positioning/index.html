Compare Competitor Positioning Without Copying It

Use public competitor messaging to identify category conventions and underserved questions.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Copying visible claims can erase distinction and repeat statements that have not been verified.

Practical workflow

  1. Choose a narrow set of direct alternatives.
  2. Record their audience, promise, proof and next step.
  3. Compare what each omits against customer questions.
  4. Write a differentiated position supported by your evidence.

Working template

Alternative: _ / audience: / promise: _ / proof: / gap: _ / our substantiated difference: _.

Applied example

If several agencies claim “full service,” compare the actual services, exclusions and evidence instead of adopting the same phrase.

Measure and review

Revisit after buyer interviews or sales objections; competitor pages are not customer research by themselves.

Guardrail

Do not repeat competitor pricing, results or credentials without verification.
