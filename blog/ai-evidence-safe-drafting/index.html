Use AI to Draft Content Without Inventing Evidence

Use AI to organize approved knowledge while making unknowns and source gaps visible.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A model cannot verify private portfolio experience that was never supplied.

A practical workflow

  1. Provide approved source notes and separate facts from ideas.
  2. Ask the draft to flag missing evidence.
  3. Verify statistics, quotes and product claims.
  4. Add genuine analysis and review before publication.

Working template

Sources: _ / confirmed facts: / evidence questions: _ / claims reviewer: / original insight: _ / disclosure: _.

Applied example

Give a draft tool approved portfolio notes and require placeholders for missing client evidence; replace each placeholder only after the owner confirms it.

Measure and review

Disclose assistance where readers may reasonably wonder how the piece was produced.

Guardrail

Never invent clients, outcomes, credentials or first-hand experience.

AI disclosure

AI tools assisted with drafting. This guide presents a general method and does not claim a verified client result.
