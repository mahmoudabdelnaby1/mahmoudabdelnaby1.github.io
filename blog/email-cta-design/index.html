PRACTICAL GUIDE · Email marketing

Give Each Email One Clear Next Action

Match the email action to its purpose and the subscriber’s current decision.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Competing links and vague buttons obscure what the email asks people to do.

Practical workflow

  1. Write the reader outcome first.
  2. Choose one primary action and label it clearly.
  3. Make secondary links less prominent.
  4. Check destination on mobile against the promise.

Working template

Purpose: _ / primary action: / button label: _ / destination: / fallback: ____.

Applied example

A renewal reminder should link to renewal details, not a homepage with several competing paths.

Measure and review

Track the intended action and outcome, not opens as a proxy.

Guardrail

Do not use misleading urgency or labels.
