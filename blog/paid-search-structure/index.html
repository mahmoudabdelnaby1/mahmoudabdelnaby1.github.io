Choose a Paid Search Structure You Can Maintain

Separate campaigns only when a real control or business difference makes separate decisions necessary.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Over-segmentation spreads limited data; under-segmentation can hide different markets, offers or goals.

A practical workflow

  1. List differences in budget, market, language, offer and outcome.
  2. Separate only where control must differ.
  3. Standardize naming, ownership and shared exclusions.
  4. Document evidence that would justify a future split.

Working template

Purpose: _ / control that differs: / shared elements: _ / naming rule: / split trigger: ____.

Applied example

If Arabic and English offers have different landing pages and budgets, separate controls may help; do not split campaigns when only spelling differs.

Measure and review

Review spend, search coverage and operator effort; ask whether another person can understand the account.

Guardrail

Avoid creating a campaign for every keyword or combining markets with different commercial constraints.
