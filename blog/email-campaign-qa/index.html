QA an Email Campaign Before Scheduling It

Check audience, links, rendering, consent and fallback behavior before sending.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·6 min read·العربية

A pre-send check should follow the message from audience selection through suppression, rendering, links, and the action after the click. A successful test send to one inbox does not verify the campaign audience or destination.

Use a release sequence

  1. Audience: confirm segment definition, consent basis, exclusions, suppression, and send count.
  2. Content: verify offer, prices, eligibility, dates, language, and every claim against the approved source.
  3. Links: open each destination on mobile; check redirects, language, UTMs, and fallback text links.
  4. Rendering: test representative email clients, Arabic direction and shaping, dark mode, and blocked images.
  5. Action: submit the form or complete the next step using a test path; confirm the event and follow-up.
  6. Ownership: confirm sender identity, reply inbox, schedule, time zone, and who can pause the send.

Send an internal proof with test labels before the real audience. Verify that test addresses cannot contaminate production reports or trigger fulfillment. For a high-risk or large send, use an approved staged rollout rather than assuming a small proof catches every issue.

Record the release

Save the approved version, audience count, reviewer, test evidence, scheduled time, and rollback owner. After sending, inspect delivery and errors promptly, then review complaints, clicks, and downstream actions with clear denominators. If a critical link or claim is wrong, use the incident process; do not send another campaign until the cause is contained.
