Check Sponsorship Disclosure in Creator Content

Make a commercial relationship clear and verify applicable current platform and local rules.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·3 min read·العربية

A sponsorship disclosure should let an ordinary viewer recognize the commercial relationship when they encounter the recommendation. A buried caption or vague tag may not do that.

Check every placement

Record each material connection: payment, gifted product, travel, affiliate commission, employment, or another benefit. Inspect video speech, on-screen text, caption, story frame, livestream, and repost separately; a label on one surface may not travel to another.

Use plain language the audience understands. Check that disclosure appears before or with the endorsement, is readable on a phone, has enough contrast and duration, and is not hidden behind “more.” For video, test with sound on and off. Plan to repeat it during live sessions for late arrivals.

Separate two reviews

The content reviewer checks whether claims are accurate and supported. The disclosure reviewer checks whether the relationship is clear and visible. Save both approvals. Confirm current platform rules and applicable local requirements with the responsible legal or compliance contact; requirements differ by market and format.

Example: a gifted product remains a material connection without a cash payment. Describe the actual arrangement rather than implying an independent review. Keep the approved script, final capture, date, placement, language, relationship, reviewer, and rule source. Recheck after translation, editing, boosting, or reposting.

This is a review aid, not legal advice.
