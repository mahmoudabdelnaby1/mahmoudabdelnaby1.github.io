Check Audience Overlap Across a Creator Shortlist

Estimate whether several creators reach distinct people or repeatedly reach the same audience.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·6 min read·العربية

Adding a second creator may expand discovery—or repeat the same sponsored message to the same people. Treat overlap as a portfolio decision, not a sum of follower counts.

Separate fit from incremental reach

For each candidate, record country, language, platform, evidence source, and capture date. “MENA” is too broad to establish fit: Egyptian Arabic, Gulf Arabic, and English-speaking professionals are different audiences. Compare the same evidence type and period. If the platform does not expose comparable audience overlap, mark it unknown instead of inventing a percentage.

One creator may bring trust in a focused community; another may add a different market, language, or format. Keep both only when the distinct role justifies added fees and production. Where data is limited, stage the campaign and test one before adding the second.

Illustrative decision

Two Arabic-speaking creators report broad regional reach. Request country breakdowns and compare the intended buyer, not language alone. If unavailable, run a small sequential test and label results directional. Do not add reported reach as unique people; platforms may count repeat exposure differently.

Record evidence, uncertainty, cost, rights, decision, and delivered results. This is a planning method, not a forecast.
