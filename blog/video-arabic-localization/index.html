Localize Video Ads for Arabic and English Audiences

Adapt dialect, examples and reading pace while preserving offer accuracy.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Subtitle translation can preserve words but miss cultural context or tone.

A practical workflow

  1. Choose dialect and register for the audience.
  2. Adapt examples rather than translating idioms literally.
  3. Verify offer and legal terms.
  4. Time captions for reading and record language reviewer.

Applied example

Measure and review

Guardrail
