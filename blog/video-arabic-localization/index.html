Localize Video Ads for Arabic and English Audiences

Adapt dialect, examples and reading pace while preserving offer accuracy.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·7 min read·العربية

Localization is not replacing English words with Arabic ones. It is preserving the offer and decision while adapting spoken register, examples, reading pace, and on-screen details for a specific audience.

Separate what must stay fixed from what can change

Keep the product facts, eligibility, price, evidence, and next action consistent. Adapt the opening phrase, idiom, voice, subtitle length, example, and currency display only after checking the target market. Label each cut with country, language register, offer version, and review date.

Illustrative example: An English line says, “Get set up in minutes.” A literal Arabic translation may sound unnatural or promise a time the process cannot guarantee. A safer Egyptian version might explain the first step in familiar terms; a Gulf-facing version may use different phrasing. Both should preserve the same verified setup steps and avoid claiming a fixed duration unless measured.

Review with a market-specific checklist

  • Is the voice clearly one register, not a mix of Egyptian and Gulf forms?
  • Do captions fit the reading pace and safe area on a phone?
  • Are currencies, dates, units, and payment references correct for the stated market?
  • Does the Arabic CTA lead to the same actual next step as the English one?
  • Has a native reviewer checked meaning, not just spelling?

Do not generalize one market’s platform rules or service conditions across MENA. Keep the original approved script and each localized version linked so a change to the offer reaches every cut.

Localization check: one message, two natural deliveries

Core meaning

“The request reached our team; we will confirm whether your location is covered.”

Neutral Arabic

Use when the audience spans several countries or the service is formal.

Local dialect

Adapt vocabulary only after confirming the target market and brand voice with a local reviewer.

What stays fixed

Coverage conditions, response promise, price, and CTA must not drift during localization.

Production check

Read aloud, time subtitles, then test meaning with a local viewer.
