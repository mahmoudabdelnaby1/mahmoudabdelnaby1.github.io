Build a Respectful Re-engagement Flow

Ask inactive subscribers whether your messages remain useful, then respect their choice.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Inactivity is a relevance signal, not permission to raise frequency indefinitely.

A practical workflow

  1. Set inactivity based on normal usage.
  2. Exclude complaints and hard bounces.
  3. Offer preference and opt-down choices.
  4. Suppress after a defined final contact.

Working template

Inactivity window: _ / exclusions: / choices: _ / opt-down: / last contact: _ / suppression date: _.

Applied example

Offer inactive subscribers a choice of monthly updates, topic preferences or unsubscribe; suppress those who do not respond after the stated final message.

Measure and review

Track restored meaningful activity, complaints and removals; list size alone is not success.

Guardrail

Honor unsubscribe requests promptly and keep suppression records.
