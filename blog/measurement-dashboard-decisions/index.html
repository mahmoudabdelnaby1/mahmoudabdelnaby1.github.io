Design a Marketing Dashboard Around Decisions

Keep only measures that help a named owner decide what to investigate or change.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A dashboard can be visually polished yet leave every reader asking what to do next.

Practical workflow

  1. List recurring decisions and owners.
  2. Map each decision to one outcome and diagnostic measure.
  3. Show definitions, date range and source.
  4. Add a note for anomalies and data freshness.

Working template

Decision: _ / owner: / outcome: _ / diagnostic: / source and refresh: _ / action threshold: _.

Applied example

If qualified inquiries fall, a diagnostic panel may compare source, response time and service fit—not just click-through rate.

Measure and review

Interview dashboard users; remove tiles never tied to a decision.

Guardrail

Keep privacy and access controls aligned with the underlying data.
