Create a Video Shot List That Protects the Message

Plan the minimum footage needed to show the user problem, process and evidence clearly.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Unplanned production can produce attractive footage that cannot support the intended story.

Practical workflow

  1. Translate each storyboard beat into a shot.
  2. List setting, action, framing and on-screen text.
  3. Capture proof and fallback takes.
  4. Check releases, privacy and brand details on set.

Working template

Scene: _ / shot: / action: _ / proof: / text: _ / release needed: _ / alternate: ____.

Applied example

For a software workflow, capture the real starting screen, key action, resulting state and one limitation in separate takes.

Measure and review

Review rough cuts against the brief before final editing.

Guardrail

Do not film identifiable people or private screens without permission.

AI disclosure
