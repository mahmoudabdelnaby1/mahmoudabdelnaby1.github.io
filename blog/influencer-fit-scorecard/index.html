PRACTICAL GUIDE · Influencer marketing

Score Creator Fit Beyond Follower Count

Assess audience relevance, content quality, execution fit and unknowns before discussing price.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·3 min read·العربية

Follower count is a weak first filter. Creator fit depends on the audience, content, market, execution, and evidence you can inspect before contracting.

Score the work, not the popularity

Use a 0–2 rating for each dimension: 0 = mismatch or no evidence; 1 = plausible but unverified; 2 = supported by a relevant sample. Weight the dimensions according to the campaign’s job; do not present the total as a universal prediction.

Dimension What to inspect before outreach
Audience fit Recent audience examples relevant to the target country and buyer task
Content fit Whether the creator can explain or demonstrate the product naturally
Market fit Language/register, location relevance, and any delivery or service constraints
Execution fit Format, turnaround, revisions, and production expectations
Evidence quality What analytics or past-work evidence is actually available, and its limits

Separate a real evidence gap from a low score. A creator who does not share private audience data may still be a fit; record the unknown instead of inventing an estimate.

Example decision

A creator with a smaller but locally relevant audience may merit a test against a larger account whose geography is unclear. Ask both for the same decision-useful evidence, such as anonymized audience geography and recent format performance. Do not use screenshots without checking date range, metric definition, and whether paid distribution was included.

Before agreement, confirm the deliverable, disclosure, usage rights, approval steps, payment, and what happens if the post is delayed. Verify current country and platform requirements with official sources and qualified counsel where needed; this scorecard is not a permit check.

Creator shortlist scorecard

Audience fit

Does the creator reach the market and eligible audience the service can actually serve?

Credibility

Does their content demonstrate the topic, or only mention it?

Conversation

Are comments relevant questions and responses, not merely a large count?

Execution

Check past delivery, disclosure, and rights clarity. Mark missing evidence unknown.

Decision

Set weights before scoring; compare fit and risk separately from follower count.
