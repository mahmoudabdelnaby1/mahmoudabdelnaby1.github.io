Map the Marketing Journey and Its Handoffs

Locate where discovery, conversion, follow-up and business outcomes connect or break.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A click can be counted while the actual inquiry is lost between teams or tools.

A practical workflow

  1. Start from a verifiable business outcome.
  2. Map each visitor action and system handoff.
  3. Name the receiving owner and failure evidence.
  4. Prioritize the narrowest broken transition.

Working template

Outcome: _ / journey step: / system: _ / owner: / failure signal: _ / recovery: _.

Applied example

A contact click can reach WhatsApp while the sales owner never sees a message; map the receipt and response steps separately from analytics.

Measure and review

Reconcile platform events with received and qualified inquiries before declaring performance.

Guardrail

A tracked event is not proof of a completed business outcome.
