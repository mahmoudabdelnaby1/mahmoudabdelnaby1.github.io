Choose Search or Social Ads by Demand and Discovery

Decide whether the task is to meet existing intent or introduce a relevant offer to an audience.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Channel comparisons fail when intent, creative burden and measurement differ.

Practical workflow

  1. Identify whether demand is explicit or latent.
  2. Check audience reach and creative readiness.
  3. Compare destination and response capacity.
  4. Set distinct hypotheses and outcome definitions.

Working template

Need: _ / demand signal: / channel role: _ / creative input: / outcome: _ / test limit: _.

Applied example

A high-intent repair query may fit search, while a new service with little query demand may need audience education first.

Measure and review

Compare qualified outcomes per market and objective, not platform-reported clicks alone.

Guardrail

Do not assume one channel universally outperforms the other.
