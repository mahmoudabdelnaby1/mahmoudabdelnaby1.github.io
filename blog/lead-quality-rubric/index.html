Create a Lead Quality Rubric Sales Can Apply

Define qualification consistently so marketing and sales distinguish intent from fit.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A large lead count can hide requests outside service scope or without enough context.

Practical workflow

  1. Choose observable fit signals tied to the offer.
  2. Define evidence levels and disqualifiers.
  3. Train reviewers on sample cases.
  4. Track disagreement and update the rubric deliberately.

Working template

Fit signal: _ / evidence source: / score: _ / disqualifier: / reviewer: _ / appeal path: _.

Applied example

A request for an unsupported region may be ineligible even if the person submitted every required form field.

Measure and review

Report inquiry, qualified, contacted and outcome stages separately.

Guardrail

Collect only data needed for the service decision; avoid sensitive or discriminatory criteria.
