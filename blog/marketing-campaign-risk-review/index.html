Run a Campaign Risk Review Before Launch

Catch broken destinations, unverified claims, missing owners and measurement gaps before spend starts.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Many launch problems are coordination failures, not creative failures.

Practical workflow

  1. Confirm audience, offer and eligibility.
  2. Verify destination, form and contact route.
  3. Test tracking and follow-up ownership.
  4. Define pause conditions and incident contact.
