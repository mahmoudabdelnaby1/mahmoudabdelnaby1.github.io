Write a Content Brief That Prevents Generic Articles

Specify the reader, task, evidence and original deliverable before drafting.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A brief that says “write about SEO” invites a summary available anywhere.

A practical workflow

  1. Define one reader and one job.
  2. Capture the question in the reader’s words.
  3. Review existing pages and primary evidence.
  4. State the unique addition, outline and honest CTA.

Working template

Reader: _ / task: / question: _ / answer gap: / evidence: _ / original contribution: _ / CTA: ____.

Applied example

For “improve WhatsApp leads,” the brief should require a funnel distinction and a tracking worksheet—not another generic list of marketing benefits.

Measure and review

Assess usefulness and evidence after drafting; no target word count substitutes for completeness.

Guardrail

Do not commission a topic solely because a keyword has volume.
