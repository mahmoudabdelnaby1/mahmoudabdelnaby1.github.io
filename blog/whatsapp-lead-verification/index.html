Measure WhatsApp Inquiries Beyond the Click

Separate a contact click from a sent message, a qualified conversation and a business outcome.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Opening WhatsApp signals intent but does not prove a message was sent or received.

A practical workflow

  1. Track the outbound click as an intent event.
  2. Confirm message receipt through an authorized source.
  3. Define qualification and response ownership.
  4. Reconcile bookings or sales separately.

Working template

Click: _ / message received: / qualified: _ / response owner: / outcome source: _ / attribution limit: _.

Applied example

A click event is intent; a sent message is contact; a qualified request needs a stated fit rule; a booking requires a separate confirmation.

Measure and review

Keep each stage distinct in reporting and avoid counting repeated clicks as multiple leads.

Guardrail

Do not infer message content from a click event.
