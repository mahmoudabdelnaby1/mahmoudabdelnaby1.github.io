Classify AI Referrals Carefully in Analytics

Separate known referrers, tagged campaigns and unattributed traffic without overclaiming source.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Private sharing, missing referrers and browser behavior can obscure how a visit began.

Practical workflow

  1. Define a maintained list of known AI referrers.
  2. Preserve referral and UTM evidence.
  3. Create a separate unknown or dark-social bucket.
  4. Audit sample landing paths and avoid retroactive certainty.

Working template

Applied example

A visit copied from an AI answer into a private chat may appear direct; preserve uncertainty instead of assigning it to a guessed engine.

Measure and review

Report identified referrals separately from estimated or unattributed visits.
