Write a Positioning Brief Before Choosing Channels

Clarify audience, problem, difference and evidence before spreading effort across platforms.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

More channels cannot repair an offer people cannot explain or distinguish.

A practical workflow

  1. Name the audience and triggering problem.
  2. Describe the current alternative.
  3. State the difference in plain language.
  4. Attach proof and name who must validate it.

Working template

For: _ / problem: / alternative: _ / difference: / evidence: _ / claim to avoid: _.

Applied example

For a measurement service, “we track everything” is vague; state which events are verified, what systems are in scope and which outcomes remain unobserved.

Measure and review

Test whether target users understand the distinction and can identify the next step.

Guardrail

Do not use superlatives or competitor comparisons without substantiation.
