Record a Marketing Baseline Before Changing Campaigns

Capture the same definitions and sources that later comparisons will use.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Without a baseline, normal variation can look like improvement or decline.

Practical workflow

  1. Choose a stable observation window.
  2. Define impressions, visits, inquiries and qualified outcomes.
  3. Record source, filters, timezone and data gaps.
  4. Save a dated snapshot before changes.

Working template

Period: _ / metric definitions: / source: _ / filters: / missing data: _ / snapshot owner: _.

Applied example

If GA4 tracking began mid-month, mark the earlier period incomplete instead of comparing raw monthly totals.

Measure and review

Compare like-for-like periods and annotate material changes or outages.

Guardrail

Do not fill missing history with estimates presented as observed data.
