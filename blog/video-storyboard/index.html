Storyboard a Short Video Around One Audience Problem

Give each scene one job and connect the opening, proof and next step.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Trying to address several audiences and promises in one short sequence makes it hard to follow.

A practical workflow

  1. Name viewer and problem.
  2. Open with recognizable context.
  3. Show one action or explanation per scene.
  4. Place proof near claims, then end with a proportionate next step.

Working template

Scene 1 problem: _ / scene 2 action: / proof: _ / limitation: / caption: _ / CTA: _.

Applied example

A 20-second software clip might show the reporting problem, one screen action, the resulting view and a limitation—not five unrelated features.

Measure and review

Read the outline without visuals for clarity; check captions and phone safe areas.

Guardrail

Keep claims proportional to the evidence and service.
