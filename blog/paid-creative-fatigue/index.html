Check Creative Fatigue Before Replacing a Winning Ad

Diagnose audience saturation, delivery changes and message wear before retiring creative.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Frequency alone does not prove fatigue; performance may shift for other reasons.

Practical workflow

  1. Compare reach, frequency and response over time.
  2. Check audience size, placement and budget changes.
  3. Review comments and creative relevance.
  4. Test a new angle while retaining the baseline.

Applied example

A stable click rate with falling qualified leads may indicate offer or audience mismatch, not creative fatigue.

Measure and review

Compare qualified outcomes and negative feedback with delivery context.

Guardrail
