Keep Video Ad and Landing Page Stories Consistent

Carry the same audience, offer and qualifying details from video into the destination.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A compelling video can create disappointment if the landing page changes the promise.

Practical workflow

  1. Record claims and qualifiers used in the video.
  2. Match page headline and offer context.
  3. Place the next action where expected.
  4. Check localized versions and destination behavior.

Working template

Applied example

If the video shows a free assessment, the page should explain eligibility and what “free” includes before the contact form.

Measure and review

Review qualified completion by creative and destination pair.

Guardrail

Do not omit conditions on the landing page that appeared in the ad.
