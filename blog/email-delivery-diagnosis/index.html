Diagnose Email Delivery Before Rewriting Copy

Separate delivery, list quality, tracking and message-fit issues before changing creative.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A click decline may start before inbox placement; rewriting subject lines first can hide the cause.

A practical workflow

  1. Separate accepted, bounced, delivered and engaged events.
  2. Inspect sender authentication and recent changes.
  3. Review list sources and complaint trends.
  4. Test one corrective action at a time.

Working template

Symptom: _ / delivery evidence: / sender setup: _ / list source: / recent change: _ / next check: _.

Applied example

A sudden bounce increase after importing a new list calls for pausing that source and inspecting consent and address quality before rewriting subject lines.

Measure and review

Use provider logs and complaint signals, not click rate alone.

Guardrail

Do not repeatedly resend to invalid or non-consenting addresses.
