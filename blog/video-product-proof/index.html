Show Product Proof Clearly in a Short Video

Demonstrate one observable task and state what the demonstration does not prove.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A polished montage can show a product without proving it solves the stated task.

Practical workflow

  1. Choose one task and starting state.
  2. Show the real interaction without skipping critical steps.
  3. Label sample data.
  4. Explain outcome, limitation and next action.

Working template

Task: _ / starting state: / action: _ / proof: / sample data?: _ / limitation: _.

Applied example

A reporting demo should show the input event, resulting record and any manual step still required.

Measure and review

Ask viewers what was demonstrated and what remains unknown.

Guardrail

Do not edit footage to imply an unverified result.
