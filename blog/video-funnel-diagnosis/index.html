Diagnose Video Ads by Funnel Stage

Locate whether the constraint is opening attention, comprehension, offer fit or action friction.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Replacing a whole video after one weak metric can discard working elements.

Practical workflow

  1. Compare early hold with later viewing.
  2. Review comments and landing behavior.
  3. Map the weak point to one stage.
  4. Change one element and preserve a comparison.

Working template

Applied example
