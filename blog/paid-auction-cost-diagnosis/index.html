Diagnose a Paid Media Cost Increase Before Changing Budget

Check auction mix, queries, placements and delivery before reacting to a rise in paid traffic cost.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A higher CPC can reflect market competition, targeting changes, placement mix or relevance—not one universal cause.

Practical workflow

  1. Compare like-for-like dates, markets and campaign types.
  2. Inspect impression share, query mix, placements and auction competition.
  3. Review recent budget, bid, creative and landing changes.
  4. Change one supported factor and watch qualified outcomes.

Working template

Metric change: _ / comparison window: / market or placement shift: _ / recent edit: / hypothesis: _ / next check: _.

Applied example

If CPC rises while qualified inquiry cost stays stable, investigate volume and quality before reducing spend; if both worsen, isolate the market or placement first.

Measure and review

Use cost alongside qualified outcomes and delivery context; annotate seasonality or major edits.

Guardrail

Do not cut a useful campaign based on CPC alone or assume a platform setting caused the shift.
