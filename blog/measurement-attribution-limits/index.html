Explain Attribution Limits in a Marketing Report

Show what the available data can connect and what remains uncertain.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Platform-reported conversions and business records may count different events and time windows.

Practical workflow

  1. Name the conversion definition and source.
  2. Record attribution window and modeled components.
  3. Reconcile with the business system where possible.
  4. Label unobserved journeys and caveats near totals.

Working template

Reported metric: _ / system: / window: _ / modeled?: / reconciliation: _ / limitation: _.

Applied example

A platform may credit a view-through conversion while CRM shows the customer already existed; report both definitions clearly.

Measure and review

Use consistent definitions across periods and avoid adding unlike platform totals together.

Guardrail

Do not present attribution as proof of causality.
