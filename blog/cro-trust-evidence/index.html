PRACTICAL GUIDE · Conversion optimization

Place Trust Evidence Where the Decision Happens

Use relevant, verifiable proof near the question it resolves.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A wall of logos or testimonials may not answer a visitor’s specific concern.

Practical workflow

  1. Identify the hesitation at each decision point.
  2. Choose proof that directly addresses it.
  3. Verify permission, date and context.
  4. Place limitations and conditions beside the evidence.

Working template

Question: _ / proof: / source: _ / permission: / date: _ / limitation: _ / placement: ____.

Applied example

A service-level claim should link to its current terms; a testimonial may not prove availability in every market.

Measure and review

Observe whether relevant questions or qualified actions change; avoid crediting proof without comparison.

Guardrail

Never fabricate reviews, customer counts or endorsements.
