Local Search for Riyadh and Jeddah Clinics: Build Accurate, Useful Branch Journeys

A local-search system for real clinic branches that connects verified profiles, location pages, appointment paths, and outcome measurement without ranking promises.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·6 min read·العربية

No process can guarantee a clinic the top position in Maps or local search. Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence, and there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. A clinic can control the accuracy of its information, the usefulness of pages for each real location, and the clarity of the appointment journey—not the searcher’s distance or a competitor’s prominence. Google: Improve your local ranking

This guide is for a clinic with a patient-facing location in Riyadh or Jeddah. It does not apply automatically to hospitals, virtual-only providers, or practices whose licensing and location status have not been verified. Review local healthcare advertising rules and professional requirements before publishing claims or offers.

Keep a separate record for each real location

Create a location record with the licensed business name, address, map pin, local phone, hours, services actually offered, booking path, accessibility and parking details where confirmed, and the person responsible for updates. Distinguish branches that share a brand. Do not create a location profile for a virtual or unstaffed office that does not meet Google’s eligibility rules, and do not add keywords to the business name.

Check that the address, pin, telephone, and hours are consistent across the Business Profile and the corresponding clinic page. If a branch moves or changes its hours, coordinate the profile and website updates so patients are not sent to an old location. Use only authentic, policy-compliant reviews; do not offer incentives or ask staff to post as patients.

Build a useful page for each patient-facing branch

When a clinic has multiple real branches, each location page should provide unique, accurate information: exact address, directions, hours, contact options, services actually available there, booking instructions, and what a patient should expect before visiting. Add a map and structured data only when they reflect visible facts. Avoid copying a single generic page and changing only “Riyadh” to “Jeddah”; that does not help a patient choose the right branch.

Healthcare content needs careful sourcing and qualified review. Identify the author or reviewer where appropriate, use current professional and regulatory information, and avoid unsupported claims about diagnosis, treatment outcomes, superiority, or guaranteed appointments. A marketing page must not replace individualized clinical advice.

Make appointment information verifiable

Test the booking path on a phone from the branch page and Business Profile. Confirm that the phone number connects to the intended team, the appointment link works, the location is clear, and the requested specialty is actually available there. If appointments are taken through WhatsApp or a call center, define what a click, conversation, booked appointment, and attended visit mean. Do not report a link opening as a confirmed appointment.

Keep patient data out of analytics events and URL parameters. Use a privacy-aware measurement design and follow applicable Saudi privacy and healthcare requirements. Limit access to booking and patient systems to authorized staff.

Measure each location without promising position

Use each location’s verified Business Profile performance and website analytics to monitor profile views, calls, direction requests, booking-link clicks, location-page visits, and confirmed appointment outcomes when those can be recorded lawfully. Separate the Riyadh and Jeddah branches. Define the period and attribution method, and reconcile digital actions with clinic operations without exposing patient details.

Rankings vary with searcher location and query. A clinic near the searcher may appear differently from the same clinic viewed across the city. Treat a rank check as a sampled observation with a query, location, device, date, and method—not a fixed position or guarantee. Follow the outcomes the clinic can control: accurate records, useful pages, working booking, and completed service capacity.

Field experiment thread: Audit one branch’s patient journey

Proposed audit plan, not a claim of ranking improvement.

1/ Select one real Riyadh or Jeddah branch and verify its eligibility and license details with the clinic owner.

2/ Check the Business Profile name, address, map pin, hours, phone, and available services against approved clinic records.

3/ Review the branch page for unique facts, correct directions, accurate service availability, and a clear booking route.

4/ Test the call and booking links on a phone; record click, conversation, booked appointment, and attendance as separate events.

5/ Remove personal health data from analytics and confirm authorized access to any patient system.

6/ Record profile actions and confirmed business outcomes for a defined period without changing unrelated branches at once.

7/ Review what improved and what cannot be inferred; do not claim a specific local ranking gain from the audit alone.

Tool-neutral prompt pack: Review a clinic’s local-search journey

Location consistency: “Compare these approved branch records [data] with the Business Profile and page details [URLs/screenshots]. Flag mismatches in name, address, phone, hours, pin, and services. Do not invent missing facts.”

Page quality: “Review the branch page [text] for unique location information, directions, appointment instructions, accessibility details, and healthcare claims needing qualified review. Do not make clinical claims or guarantee search visibility.”

Measurement map: “Define separate events for profile click, call connection, booking start, confirmed appointment, and attendance. Exclude patient health information and list consent, access, and retention checks for a privacy review.”

Local-rank sampling: “Create a repeatable sampling log with query, searcher location, device, date, and method. Explain why results may vary and do not treat a sample as a permanent rank.”

Stop-doing swipe file: Replace local-ranking promises

Stop: “We guarantee the clinic will rank first in Riyadh.” Use: “We will improve the verified profile and patient journey, then report sampled visibility and confirmed actions.”

Stop: Create a profile for every neighborhood name. Use: “Represent eligible, real patient-facing locations only.”

Stop: Copy the same page and swap the city. Use: “Publish accurate facts unique to each real branch.”

Stop: “A map click is an appointment.” Use: “Track click, connection, booking, and attendance separately.”

Stop: Ask staff to post reviews as patients. Use: “Request authentic reviews without incentives and follow current platform rules.”

Stop: Add patient data to analytics URLs. Use: “Keep sensitive health information out of tracking and follow privacy requirements.”
