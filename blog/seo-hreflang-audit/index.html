Audit Arabic and English Hreflang Pairs

Check that translated pages identify equivalent routes and each locale links back consistently.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A language switcher alone does not tell search engines which pages are equivalents.

Practical workflow

  1. List canonical URL pairs.
  2. Check reciprocal hreflang values and language codes.
  3. Confirm each canonical points to its own preferred URL.
  4. Test x-default policy and page availability.

Working template

Applied example

Pair the English service guide only with its corresponding Arabic guide, not the Arabic homepage because it is in another language.

Measure and review

Recheck sitemap alternates and rendered page head after changes.

Guardrail

Do not label unrelated or partially translated pages as exact equivalents.
