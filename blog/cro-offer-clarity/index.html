Clarify an Offer Before Redesigning the Whole Page

Resolve uncertainty about price, eligibility, inclusions and next steps before cosmetic changes.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

A redesign cannot answer a commercial question the page leaves unstated.

A practical workflow

  1. Ask recent visitors and sales staff what was unclear.
  2. List offer, inclusions, conditions and exclusions.
  3. Rewrite the first-screen explanation.
  4. Validate the wording with the offer owner before testing.

Working template

Offer: _ / included: / excluded: _ / eligibility: / lead sentence: _ / next step: _.

Applied example

A consultation page should say duration, audience, included preparation and whether a fee applies before asking visitors to submit contact details.

Measure and review

Observe qualified actions and follow-up questions after the clarification.

Guardrail

Commercial and legal owners must confirm terms before publication.
