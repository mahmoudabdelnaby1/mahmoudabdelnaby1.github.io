Repurpose One Idea for Different Social Formats

Keep the useful idea while adapting the opening, depth and action to each channel.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·7 min read·العربية

Repurposing should preserve the idea and change the experience. Copying the same caption to every platform keeps the words but loses the format, context, and reason a person uses that channel.

Adapt one source idea deliberately

Start with one verified idea, its evidence, audience, and next action. Then rebuild the opening and format for each channel. Do not invent a new claim to make the short version more exciting.

Source idea: A bilingual service site should explain the next step after a WhatsApp click.

  • LinkedIn: A short operational post: “A chat-button click is not a received inquiry.” Show the four events the team should separate: click, opened chat, sent message, qualified request. End with a measurement question.
  • Short video: Show a phone screen with the visitor tapping, editing the prefilled message, and deciding whether to send it. Use captions; do not show personal data.
  • Instagram carousel: One slide per stage, with the handoff owner named on the final slide.
  • Email: Share a small QA checklist for the contact path and link to the complete guide.

These are format sketches, not ready-to-post claims about platform reach.

Keep the evidence attached

Maintain a source card with the original statement, evidence, date checked, language version, approved wording, and limits. If the evidence changes, update or pause every derived version. For Arabic, transcreate the example for the target audience; do not combine Egyptian and Gulf idioms in one asset just to appear broadly regional.

Measure each adaptation by its job

A video may aim to make the process understandable; a carousel may aim to help a manager audit it; an email may help someone complete it. Set a different success event for each format. Views and reposts show distribution, not understanding or business outcome.
