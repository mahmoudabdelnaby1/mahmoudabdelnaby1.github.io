Choose a Conversion Signal That Matches the Campaign Goal

Align optimization events with what the campaign can verify and the business values.

Mahmoud Abdelnaby·2 min read·العربية

Optimizing for a convenient event can produce activity without the intended outcome.

Practical workflow

  1. Define the true business outcome.
  2. Map observable events from intent to result.
  3. Choose the deepest reliable signal with enough volume.
  4. Keep quality and volume guardrails visible.

Working template

Business goal: _ / available events: / reliability: _ / selected signal: / quality check: ____.

Applied example

If bookings are too sparse for learning, a verified inquiry may be an interim signal—but review booking quality separately.

Measure and review

Reconcile platform event counts with the receiving system and qualified outcome.

Guardrail

Do not optimize to an unverified click when the goal is a completed inquiry.
