# Marketing Systems Lab — Research-stage concept register

This directory contains a bilingual static catalogue of **89 distinct digital marketing tool concepts** from the 26 October 2026 intake. Three overlapping concept pairs were consolidated before publication.

**Scope:** these entries are research candidates, not a claim that 89 products have been built. The intake did not independently verify that the proposed products work, market demand, licenses, external integrations, or production readiness. No candidate is presented here as released software.

The public catalogue lists concepts by canonical name and the marketing areas they touch. It intentionally excludes unverified opportunity scores and build/test claims.
