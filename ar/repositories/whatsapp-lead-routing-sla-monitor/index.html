Build an open-source WhatsApp lead routing and SLA monitoring toolkit: 1) System architecture blueprint for automated round-robin lead assignment with working hours and capacity rules, 2) Python webhook simulation engine (lead_router.py) tracking message timestamps and flagging SLA breaches (>15 min), 3) Slack/Telegram notification templates for abandoned leads.

النطاق والقيود

  • Business Hours SLA Windows: Calculates response time latencies exclusively during active working hours, excluding overnight delays from agent performance metrics.
  • Round-Robin & Skill Routing: Distributes inbound chats across sales agents based on territory (KSA vs UAE vs Egypt) and product knowledge.
  • Webhook Queue Ingestion: Processes incoming Meta WhatsApp message status webhooks with deduplication to ensure accurate first-response timestamps.

هذه الصفحة تلخص التوثيق المتاح ولا تثبت أداءً أو نتيجة تجارية. تحقق من صلاحية البيانات والمتطلبات قبل الاستخدام.
