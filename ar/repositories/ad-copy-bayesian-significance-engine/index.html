دليل المستودع: Ad Copy Split-Test Bayesian Significance Engine

صفحة تعريفية مستقلة بمستودع Ad Copy Split-Test Bayesian Significance Engine. توضّح ما الذي يحاول حله، ولمن يناسب، وما الملفات التي ستجدها قبل الانتقال إلى المصدر.

المشكلة التي يعالجها

يوضح التوثيق الأصلي نطاق المشكلة بهذا الوصف:

Develop a zero-dependency Python script (bayesian_ad_tester.py) that computes Bayesian posterior probabilities for multi-variant creative tests (Beta-Binomial conjugate model for CTR and conversion rates). Features: 1) Calculates probability of variant A outperforming B, 2) Expected loss calculation (risk of being wrong), 3) Minimum sample size recommendation, 4) Generates a standalone visual HTML report.

النطاق والقيود

  • Bayesian vs Frequentist Framing: Reports posterior probability of winning rather than confusing p-values, preventing premature test stopping.
  • Informative Prior Calibration: Uses user-supplied priors; choose them from relevant account history rather than a claimed regional average.
  • Multi-Armed Bandit Integration: Calculates expected loss in conversions if the currently leading ad variant is chosen immediately.
