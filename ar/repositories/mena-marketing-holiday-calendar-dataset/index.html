المشكلة التي يعالجها

يوضح التوثيق الأصلي نطاق المشكلة بهذا الوصف:

Build an open-source MENA commercial and cultural holiday dataset (2025-2028): 1) Machine-readable JSON and CSV detailing dates, shopping phase duration, and recommended creative lead times for all major Islamic and national holidays (Saudi National Day, Saudi Foundation Day, UAE National Day, Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, White Friday, Mother's Day, Back-to-School), 2) Importable .ics calendar file, 3) 52-week marketing flighting schedule.

النطاق والقيود

  • Lunar Calendar Sightings: Islamic holidays (Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha) depend on lunar moon sightings; dates require documented operational sighting buffers.
  • National Days & Civil Gazettes: Incorporates fixed statutory civil holidays (Saudi National Day, Saudi Founding Day, UAE National Day, Egypt Revolution Day).
  • Commercial Retail Seasons: Maps major promotional buying seasons (Back to School, White Friday, Yellow Friday, Ramadan retail surge).
