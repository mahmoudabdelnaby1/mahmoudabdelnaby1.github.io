المشكلة التي يعالجها

يوضح التوثيق الأصلي نطاق المشكلة بهذا الوصف:

Develop a comprehensive governance framework for Meta Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns: 1) Account-level existing customer audience definition protocol (email lists, 180-day purchasers, CRM data), 2) Existing customer budget cap guidelines (with configurable budget guardrails to be chosen from account-specific evidence), 3) Geographically isolated holdout test design to measure true incremental revenue lift, 4) 20-point campaign launch checklist.

النطاق والقيود

  • Existing Customer Budget Caps: ASC campaigns risk burning budget on brand loyals; should be evaluated with account-level incrementality evidence and campaign settings.
  • Creative Asset Overlap: Meta's algorithmic asset combinations can generate disjointed Arabic headlines and descriptions if not grouped into strict theme sets.
  • Attribution Reality Check: ASC heavily claims 1-day view conversions; media buyers must audit incrementality against 7-day click attribution.
