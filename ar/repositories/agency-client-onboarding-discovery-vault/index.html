النطاق والقيود

  • Zero Shared Password Policy: Mandates role-based enterprise access via Meta Business Portfolio, Google MCC, and TikTok Business Center rather than raw logins.
  • Brand Asset Intake Governance: Systematic collection of vector logos, brand books, typography licenses, and past campaign performance data.
  • Stakeholder SLA Alignment: Establishes clear feedback turnaround SLAs and change request protocols during the first 30 days of client engagement.
