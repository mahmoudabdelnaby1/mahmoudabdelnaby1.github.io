النطاق والقيود

  • Packaging Component Architecture: Blueprints packaging inserts, custom QR code landing pages, and branded physical touchpoints.
  • Referral Incentive Framing: Designs two-sided reward mechanics (e.g. SAR 50 for the referee, SAR 50 for the advocate) to optimize viral loop k-factor.
  • Post-Purchase Review Timing: Sequences digital review requests to trigger after a configurable interval following confirmed delivery; test timing with customer feedback and local rules.
