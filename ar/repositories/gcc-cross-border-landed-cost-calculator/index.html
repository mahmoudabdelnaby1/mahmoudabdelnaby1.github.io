المشكلة التي يعالجها

يوضح التوثيق الأصلي نطاق المشكلة بهذا الوصف:

Build an open-source cross-border GCC landed cost calculator: 1) Single-file responsive HTML/JS tool (landed_cost.html) estimating landed costs from configurable assumptions; tax and duty treatment varies by destination, product classification, origin, and shipment, so users must verify current rates, 2) Excel master pricing workbook, 3) DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) customer checkout disclosure copy swipe file.

النطاق والقيود

  • Unified GCC Customs Tariff: Uses a fixed 5% customs-duty scenario for illustration; actual rates depend on tariff classification, origin, destination, and exemptions.
  • VAT Inbound Assessment: Uses destination VAT assumptions for an estimate. Import VAT bases and applicable rates depend on current law and shipment details; verify with the relevant authority or broker.
  • Estimate Limits: Rates are fixed examples rather than live tariff lookups. Product codes, origin rules, exemptions, de minimis rules, and courier charges can change the actual total.

هذه الصفحة تلخص التوثيق المتاح ولا تثبت أداءً أو نتيجة تجارية. تحقق من صلاحية البيانات والمتطلبات قبل الاستخدام.
