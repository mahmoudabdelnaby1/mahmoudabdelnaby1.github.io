المشكلة التي يعالجها

يوضح التوثيق الأصلي نطاق المشكلة بهذا الوصف:

Develop a pure Python influencer audit utility (pod_detector.py): 1) Ingests exported comment CSVs (Author, Text, Timestamp), 2) Calculates Lexical Diversity (Shannon Entropy) to spot repetitive canned phrases, 3) Detects comment timing bursts (50 comments in 2 minutes = pod coordination), 4) Outputs an authenticity score (0-100) and visual HTML diagnostic report.

النطاق والقيود

  • Engagement Pod Detection: Flags repetitive, generic comments ('روعة', 'ما شاء الله', emoji strings) posted within minutes of publishing.
  • Follower Spike Telemetry: Identifies unnatural overnight follower increases inconsistent with typical organic discovery curves.
  • Audience Geography Audit: Measures percentage of followers located outside the target GCC/MENA market.
