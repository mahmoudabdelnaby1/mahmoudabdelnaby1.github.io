النطاق والقيود

  • Mawthooq Platform Mandate: All individuals publishing commercial advertising on social platforms in KSA must hold an active Mawthooq permit.
  • Commercial Disclosure Placement: Sponsored posts must explicitly display the statutory ad disclosure badge (#إعلان or #مادة_إعلانية) prominently.
  • Sanctions & Penalties Framework: Outlines legal repercussions for advertising unlicensed financial, medical, or real estate products under GAMR regulations.
