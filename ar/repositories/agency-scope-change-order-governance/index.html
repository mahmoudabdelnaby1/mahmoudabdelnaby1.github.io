النطاق والقيود

  • Scope Creep Containment: Establishes clear boundary definitions between included retainer deliverables and billable out-of-scope work.
  • Formal Change Order Workflow: Requires documented impact assessments (timeline extension + cost adjustment) before initiating out-of-scope tasks.
  • Client Sign-Off Audit Trail: Implements binding digital sign-off workflows to prevent invoice disputes during project delivery.
