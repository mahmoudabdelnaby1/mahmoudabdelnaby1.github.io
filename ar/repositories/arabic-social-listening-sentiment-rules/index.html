النطاق والقيود

  • Sarcasm & Dialectal Inversion: Accounts for common Arabic sarcastic expressions where surface positive words convey negative sentiment.
  • Regional Morphology Handling: Distinguishes between Egyptian (مش حلو), Gulf (مو زين), and Levant (مو منيح) negation structures.
  • Auditable Heuristic Ruleset: Rule definitions run locally using regex and token lookups without requiring black-box paid APIs.
