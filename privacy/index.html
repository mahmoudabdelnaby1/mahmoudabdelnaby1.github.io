Privacy and analytics choices

This page explains the optional analytics used on this portfolio and how to change your choice. Analytics are off unless you choose “Accept optional analytics” in the privacy panel.

What the optional tools do

With your permission, Google Analytics (GA4) measures visits, traffic sources, page views, and selected interactions. Microsoft Clarity provides heatmaps and session recordings to help understand page use. The site does not send form contents, WhatsApp message contents, phone numbers, or prefilled message text to these analytics tools.

A click on a WhatsApp link may be counted as a contact intent after consent. It does not show that WhatsApp opened, a message was sent, or an inquiry was qualified.

Your choice

Choose accept or reject in the privacy panel. The choice is stored in this browser so it can be remembered. You can reopen the panel using “Privacy settings” at the bottom of the page. Rejecting stops optional analytics from loading. If you withdraw after accepting, the site sends denial signals to tools already loaded; data already collected may remain subject to the providers’ policies.

Cookies and providers

The site’s consent panel uses browser storage only to remember your choice. If you accept, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity may use their own storage and process data under their settings and policies. Clarity’s consent mode is enabled for this project. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact option on the portfolio. Avoid sending sensitive information through public links.
