01

When to use it

Build a client proposal from actual delivery scope, time, costs, and responsibilities.

02

Prepare these inputs

Team hours and loaded cost, direct expenses, responsibilities, workload, term, and agreed service boundaries.

03

A practical workflow

  1. 01

    Confirm the question and the decision this workflow should support.

  2. 02

    Replace example fields with current account or business inputs.

  3. 03

    Follow the review steps and record sources, dates, and unknowns.

  4. 04

    Estimate resources per deliverable, calculate from chosen assumptions, and review scope, exclusions, and obligations.

  5. 05

    Compare outcomes using a consistent baseline and definition.

04

What the toolkit contains

A bilingual proposal template, blank cost inputs, and negotiable clauses for media spend and service levels.

05

Review boundaries

No market rate or recommended margin is asserted. Examples are not client engagements; have contract and tax terms reviewed by qualified professionals.
