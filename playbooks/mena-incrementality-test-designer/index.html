Marketing Incrementality Test Design

Decide whether a controlled marketing test is feasible, document the design before launch, and interpret results within their evidence limits.

Open toolkit on GitHub ↗All playbooks
01

When to use it

You need to assess whether a proposed test can support a claim that marketing caused additional business outcomes.

02

What to prepare

A defined decision, outcome and system of record, population, intervention, comparison, and market and timing context.

03

How to use the framework

  1. 01

    Write the decision and causal question in one sentence.

  2. 02

    Check that treatment and comparison can be assigned, measured, and kept sufficiently separate.

  3. 03

    Record the method, outcome, analysis, timing, risks, and decision rules before launch.

  4. 04

    At the planned end, document deviations, uncertainty, the decision, and evidence limits.

04

What the toolkit includes

A feasibility framework, preregistration template, context and contamination register, results readout template, and design-review scenarios.

05

Limits and review

Attribution alone does not establish causality. A before/after comparison is not automatically an experiment. Verify current platform mechanics and scope conclusions to the tested population, intervention, outcome, and window.
