When to use it

Review product data before Merchant Center submission and reconcile identifiers, prices, availability, and landing pages.

02

Prepare these inputs

Current feed export, SKU and available GTIN or MPN, currency, price, stock, target country, and product URL.

03

A practical workflow

  1. 01

    Confirm the question and the decision this workflow should support.

  2. 02

    Replace example fields with current account or business inputs.

  3. 03

    Follow the review steps and record sources, dates, and unknowns.

  4. 04

    Keep a source copy, reconcile each item against store records, then address current Merchant Center diagnostics by root cause.

  5. 05

    Compare outcomes using a consistent baseline and definition.

04

What the toolkit contains

A workflow for matching product data to the source, landing page, checkout, and current diagnostics.

05

Review boundaries

Never invent an identifier, price, or specification. Requirements vary by item, destination, and current data specification; this guide cannot guarantee approval.
