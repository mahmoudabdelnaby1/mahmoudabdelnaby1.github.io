Prepare these inputs

A practical workflow

  1. 01

    Provide source copy and verified product or offer facts.

  2. 02

    Set the audience, market, platform, format and brand voice.

  3. 03

    Review localized options for meaning and market fit before publishing.

04

What the toolkit contains

A localization workflow, reusable prompt templates, vocabulary and register references, and illustrative rewrites.

05

Review boundaries

Ask a fluent local reviewer to check naturalness and fit. Never invent product benefits, discounts, availability, delivery promises, or results.
