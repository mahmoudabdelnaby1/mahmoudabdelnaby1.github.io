STOREFRONT SEARCH QA

Salla & Zid Storefront SEO Review

Review search fields and visible product, category, and content pages on Salla or Zid, tying every proposed change to a verified fact and a human review.

Open the toolkit ↗All playbooks
01

When to use it

When a Salla or Zid team needs to review search titles, descriptions, and page content across products, categories, the homepage, or editorial pages.

02

Prepare these inputs

Platform and admin version, language and market, page URLs, approved product/offer/stock facts, and any relevant Search Console evidence.

03

A practical review workflow

Start with a defined sample and record the URL, language, and current fields. Compare them with the public page and approved facts. Draft page-specific changes, review one representative save on the public site before scaling, and log sources, owners, approval, risks, and rollback details.

04

What the toolkit includes

A platform field map by page type, a fillable review register, a pre-publication checklist, and human-reviewed AI-assisted usage instructions.

05

Review boundaries

This workflow cannot guarantee crawling, indexing, rankings, rich results, AI citations, or sales. Do not change an established URL or crawler control without approval and a review plan. It does not provide injectable schema.

STEPS

A repeatable sequence

  1. 01

    Define the sample, page types, language, and admin version.

  2. 02

    Record the current URL and fields, visible page content, fact source, and source date.

  3. 03

    Review accuracy, language, page uniqueness, and whether the page helps a real visitor.

  4. 04

    Draft clear page-specific fields from approved facts; do not invent price, delivery, or availability claims.

  5. 05

    Treat URL and crawler-control changes as sensitive: document impact and rollback, and obtain required approval.

  6. 06

    Save a representative edit and inspect the public URL, render, language, and internal links.

  7. 07

    Use Search Console to observe indexing, impressions, and clicks as separate signals, not as a guaranteed business outcome.

  8. 08

    Log changes, owner, reviewer, follow-up date, and unresolved issues.
