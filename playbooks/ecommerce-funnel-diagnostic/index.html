E-commerce Funnel Diagnostic

Find and validate where a store journey loses progress before choosing a page, checkout, or payment change.

Open toolkit on GitHub ↗All playbooks
01

When to use it

Traffic, leads, or orders change and the team needs to distinguish a measured journey issue from a guess.

02

Prepare these inputs

Defined period and timezone, event definitions, store orders and statuses, consent and deduplication context, and usable segment data.

03

A practical workflow

  1. 01

    State what changed, when, and for which source and segment; keep platform attribution distinct from store orders.

  2. 02

    Validate event definitions, currency, deduplication, consent, checkout transitions, and reconciliation to orders.

  3. 03

    Map comparable populations through landing, product, cart, checkout, payment, order, and fulfillment stages where available.

  4. 04

    Segment only when definitions and sample sizes allow a useful comparison; mark weak evidence inconclusive.

  5. 05

    Reproduce the customer path on mobile and desktop and record observed friction with steps or screenshots.

  6. 06

    Choose one evidence-based change with a primary metric, guardrails, owner, review date, and rollback condition.

04

What the toolkit contains

A funnel evidence log and a diagnostic decision record for hypotheses, tests, guardrails, limitations, and decisions.

05

Review boundaries

There are no universal conversion thresholds in this toolkit. It complements unit-economics modeling and Arabic RTL usability review.
