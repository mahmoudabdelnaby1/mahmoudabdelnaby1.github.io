Marketing Workflow QA & Evidence Gates

Use these review gates to connect campaign, content, commerce, measurement and customer operations before launch—and to record what was actually verified.

Use this guide as a practical framework, then verify changing details against current primary sources before making a decision.

Open toolkit on GitHub ↗All playbooks
01

When to use it

Several teams or channels contribute to one customer journey, and a checklist tied to evidence and owners is needed before a change goes live.

02

Prepare these inputs

Decision and owner; audience, offer and markets; channel/asset versions; destination pages; consent and data flows; event definitions; operational handoffs; source links and change dates.

03

A practical workflow

  1. 01

    Decision and claims: state the intended decision, evidence, assumptions and review owner. Separate a hypothesis from a measured result.

  2. 02

    Campaign and links: align objective, audience, offer, budget assumptions, landing page and tagged URLs; check redirects and market/language parity.

  3. 03

    Creative and content: confirm factual claims, rights, version approval, platform fit, accessibility and destination consistency.

  4. 04

    Experience and commerce: complete the main mobile journey, validation, price/promotion, stock, checkout and recovery paths with real test cases.

  5. 05

    Measurement and privacy: map browser/server events, consent, identifiers, deduplication and order/CRM outcomes; record which platform diagnostics and backend records were checked.

  6. 06

    Lifecycle and operations: verify opt-in, suppression, frequency, routing, ownership and escalation for email, messaging and automation.

  7. 07

    Search and discovery: check visible page facts, metadata, language routing, canonical/hreflang and crawler rules without changing established URLs or bot access casually.

  8. 08

    Launch and learning: record owner, timestamp, evidence link, unresolved risk, rollback/stop condition and post-launch check. Close only the items with recorded evidence.

04

What the toolkit contains

A cross-functional review register with decision, owner, evidence, state, risk and follow-up fields; plus eight gates for campaign, creative, commerce, measurement, lifecycle and discovery work. It complements specialist playbooks and does not replace platform, legal, privacy or engineering review.

05

Review boundaries

A checked box is not proof by itself. Attach a dated source, screenshot, test record or system output and state its limits. Do not claim launch success, compliance or production readiness from a static checklist.

INTEGRATED PRACTICE

Specialist workflows this guide connects

01

Measurement, attribution & consent

Define events and outcomes before implementation; check consent, deduplication, backend reconciliation and uncertainty.

02

Search & AI discovery

Record query, market, language, date and visible citations; review crawl access and language parity without promising rankings.

03

Campaign operations & governance

Align objective, audience, offer, landing page, budget assumptions, tagged URL, owner, claim evidence and stop condition.

04

Creative, content & rights

Approve factual claims, asset version, rights, audience fit, language, CTA and destination together.

05

Commerce, feeds & conversion

Reconcile product facts; check price, stock, promotions, mobile checkout, returns and settled orders.

06

Arabic localization & parity

Compare the same promise, offer, CTA, URL and measurement across languages with a local reviewer and RTL task checks.

07

Email, lifecycle & automation

Check permission, suppression, frequency, provider evidence, triggers, routing, ownership and human escalation.

08

Journey, landing pages & CRO

Start with customer friction, one hypothesis and a primary measure; test the full mobile path and do not overstate low-volume tests.

09

Integrations & technical operations

Verify provider contract, access, field mapping, failure/retry behavior, data minimization, monitoring and recovery ownership.

10

Reporting & evidence governance

Declare metric definitions, dates and attribution windows; separate observed, attributed, modeled and experimentally measured outcomes.
