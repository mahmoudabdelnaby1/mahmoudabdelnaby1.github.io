ORGANIC CONTENT OPERATIONS

Organic Content & Video Operations Planner

Connect audience needs, editorial choices, production, permissions, publishing, and learning across organic social and video.

Open toolkit on GitHub ↗All playbooks
01

When to use it

A team needs an end-to-end workflow for useful organic posts and video, separate from paid creator authorization.

02

Prepare these inputs

Audience questions, business goal, approved facts and claims, brand voice, channel, source assets, rights, reviewers, and metric definitions.

03

A practical workflow

  1. 01

    Choose an audience question and state what the content should help someone understand or do.

  2. 02

    Select a content job and channel-native format; set the editorial mix from the goal and evidence, not a universal ratio.

  3. 03

    Write a brief with one message, sources, sequence, CTA, accessibility needs, and claims to avoid.

  4. 04

    Plan the source asset, edits, crops, language, captions, reviewers, and derivative formats.

  5. 05

    Confirm permissions for people, music, locations, customer material, and intended usage before publishing.

  6. 06

    Record the live link and compare outcomes using consistent definitions; document limitations and the next iteration.

04

What the toolkit contains

A reusable editorial/video brief and a repurposing matrix for channel, format, language, rights, approval, and measurement.

05

Review boundaries

Do not invent testimonials or performance results. This planner does not promise reach, virality, engagement, or lower acquisition cost.
