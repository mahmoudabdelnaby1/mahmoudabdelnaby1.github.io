01

When to use it

Design a property inquiry journey connecting customer requirements to a viewing or sales handoff.

02

Prepare these inputs

Verified available units, locations and prices, qualification rules, approved financing options, viewing hours, and CRM.

03

A practical workflow

  1. 01

    Confirm the question and the decision this workflow should support.

  2. 02

    Replace example fields with current account or business inputs.

  3. 03

    Follow the review steps and record sources, dates, and unknowns.

  4. 04

    Collect only necessary fields; distinguish inquiry, qualified lead, and completed viewing in CRM records.

  5. 05

    Compare outcomes using a consistent baseline and definition.

04

What the toolkit contains

Search-intent prompts, qualification questions, WhatsApp scripts, and a blank cost-per-viewing input sheet.

05

Review boundaries

Remove unsupported investment or legal promises. Do not populate calculations with assumed market costs or fabricated case outcomes.
