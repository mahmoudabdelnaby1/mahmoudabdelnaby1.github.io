01LOCALIZATION

Saudi and Gulf Arabic Copy Localizer

Use it when
A campaign needs natural, locally appropriate Arabic rather than literal translation.
Prepare
Country, audience, channel, formality, brand voice, verified product facts, offer terms, and wording to avoid.
What it includes
A localization workflow, reusable prompt templates, vocabulary and register references, and illustrative rewrites.
Review
Ask a fluent local reviewer to check naturalness and fit. Never invent product benefits, discounts, availability, delivery promises, or results.

02COMMERCE ECONOMICS

E-commerce Unit Economics

Use it when
You need to estimate what remains from an order after fulfilment, payment, advertising, delivery outcomes, and returns.
Prepare
Your current product and fulfilment costs, payment and advertising fees, tax treatment, COD share, delivery success, and return assumptions; record sources and dates.
What it includes
A blank input template and formula framework for contribution per order and break-even acquisition cost. It is not a pre-built spreadsheet calculator.
Review
Confirm tax and provider fees with qualified sources, then reconcile estimates to settled orders and cash received.

03MEASUREMENT

E-commerce Event Tracking Implementation and QA

Use it when
You are mapping or diagnosing browser and server-side commerce events across a storefront and ad platforms.
Prepare
The real data layer, event names, consent state, event IDs, timestamps, currency, order lifecycle, and platform setup.
What it includes
Event-mapping schemas, implementation and deduplication checklists, platform notes, and a blank evidence and reconciliation report.
Review
Test each platform and compare with backend orders. Server-side tracking does not guarantee restored signals or attribution.

FROM INPUTS TO A DECISION

How to use a playbook

Choose for the decision you need to make, not just by the tool name.

  1. 01

    Name the decision

    Write the question you need answered and the decision the output should support.

  2. 02

    Bring real inputs

    Use current store, offer, market, and account data; record where it came from and when.

  3. 03

    Check assumptions

    Verify changing details in official documentation and involve a qualified reviewer when needed.

  4. 04

    Measure the result

    Compare with an appropriate baseline before attributing an outcome to the change.

CLEAR ANSWERS BEFORE YOU START

Questions people ask

Is this an AI platform?

No. This is a set of reusable written workflows and templates. It does not host or run AI models.

Is the e-commerce worksheet a ready-made calculator?

No. It is a blank input template and formula framework. Add your own figures, calculate, and review before making financial decisions.

Does the advertising review approve an ad legally?

No. It organizes a human review; it does not replace a regulator or qualified local counsel.

Do the playbooks guarantee better campaign results?

No. Outcomes depend on the market, offer, execution, and measurement. Treat examples as hypotheses and measure actual results.
