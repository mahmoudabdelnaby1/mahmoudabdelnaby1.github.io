Public Ad Creative Review: Evidence-Led Competitor Research

Use public advertising examples to document visible creative patterns and questions for your own brief—without inferring private performance or treating an example as a proven winner.

Use this guide as a practical framework, then verify changing details against current primary sources before making a decision.

01

When to use it

You are preparing a campaign brief and need a time-stamped, market-specific view of publicly visible competitor messaging, formats or landing-page choices.

02

Prepare these inputs

Research question, market and date range, relevant advertiser/category, public library URL, capture date, visible creative and destination details, and your own campaign objective.

03

A practical workflow

  1. 01

    Define a narrow question before browsing, such as how offers are framed or which proof points are made visible.

  2. 02

    Search the public ad library manually and record the exact URL, advertiser/page, market filter and date observed.

  3. 03

    Describe only what is visible: opening hook, offer, format, proof, CTA, language and destination. Keep interpretation in a separate field.

  4. 04

    Compare examples by message and audience context, not by assumed reach, spend, conversion or profitability.

  5. 05

    Convert observations into testable questions for your own audience and offer; do not copy protected assets or distinctive expression.

  6. 06

    Recheck the public listing before using the note because ads, availability and interface details can change.

04

What the toolkit contains

A manual observation log, creative-pattern coding sheet, evidence/interpretation split, brief-question generator and review checklist. No scraping, automated tracking, performance estimates or API claims are included.

05

Review boundaries

Public availability does not establish permission to reuse creative work. Respect platform terms and applicable rights; cite the public source and avoid collecting personal data.
