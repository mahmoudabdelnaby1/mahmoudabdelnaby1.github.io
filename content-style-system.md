# Bilingual Article Style System

## Publishing rule

Every article keeps the six formats requested for the core library:

1. Short-video script
2. Educational carousel
3. Case-study or experiment thread (a test plan when no real result is documented)
4. Tool-neutral prompt pack
5. Stop-doing swipe file
6. Hook–Value–Template

Each article also receives one additional format selected for its subject and reader task. The advanced format is chosen from a broader library so the 100-topic collection offers useful variety without pasting every optional section into every page.

## Advanced format library

| Format | Use when the reader needs to… | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Decision map | choose an action based on a verified condition | Keep branches tied to the source; route missing evidence to verification |
| Troubleshooting runbook | trace a symptom through records and handoffs | Separate observed state from diagnosis; include a repeatable check |
| Worked scenario | see how a method applies from input to review | Label it hypothetical unless it is a permissioned, sourced case |
| Evidence scorecard | assess readiness or quality consistently | Use yes/no/unknown; require a record for every positive judgment |
| Annotated teardown | understand why a sample line, workflow, or asset needs revision | Label editorial examples; do not imply a real customer quote |
| Pre-launch gate | catch failures before a campaign or workflow goes live | Include a stop condition, owner, and fallback |
| Implementation playbook | carry a method into an owned workflow | State the deliverable and what must be logged; do not assume a result |
| Reader questions and direct answers | resolve practical follow-up questions quickly | Answer only from the article’s supported material; mark unknowns |
| Failure-mode pre-mortem | anticipate how an approach could fail | Describe risks to check, never as events that already happened |

## Topic matching

The generator groups the existing article themes by subject and rotates only through relevant formats: strategy and journey articles use decision maps or scenarios; measurement and SEO articles use runbooks and evidence scorecards; content and social articles use teardowns, playbooks, and reader questions; video articles use teardowns and launch gates; AI and automation articles use pre-mortems, runbooks, and launch gates. Assignment is deterministic, so rebuilding the site preserves each article’s selected format.

## Editorial safeguards

- Use the article’s own source material, examples, and steps; do not add search-volume claims or invented client metrics.
- Distinguish a hypothesis, illustration, sourced fact, and measured result.
- Do not append a standard AI-drafting disclosure footer. Keep factual attribution and source provenance where relevant to the claim.
- Update the displayed reading time when the article changes.
- Treat format as a way to serve the reader’s intent, not as a promise of search ranking, indexing, or AI citation.
- Do not add sections that repeat a point without giving the reader another usable decision, check, or artifact.

## Research basis

- Google Search Central recommends original, useful, people-first content and warns against scaled pages that add little value. Its guidance for AI features says the ordinary technical and content foundations still apply; no special page format or markup guarantees inclusion. [Helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) · [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)
- Wistia’s 2026 report draws on a survey of more than 900 professionals and over 13 million hosted videos; it reports strong engagement for educational and tutorial videos on its platform. This supports keeping clear tutorials in the format mix, not a claim that a specific length or script will win everywhere. [Wistia State of Video 2026](https://wistia.com/blog/video-marketing-statistics)
- Content Marketing Institute’s 2025 B2B survey found marketers often rated video and case studies among effective formats. These are reported marketer perceptions, not guaranteed outcomes for this portfolio or a MENA-specific sample. [CMI B2B content marketing research](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/b2b-research/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research-2025)
- Current GEO research describes inconsistent terminology and measurement across platforms. Track citations as dated, query-specific observations rather than a stable ranking promise. [Critical survey of GEO research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14035)
