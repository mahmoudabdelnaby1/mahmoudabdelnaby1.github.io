Social Media Brand Crisis Response & Escalation Decision Tree

De-escalate viral social media crises before they ruin your brand reputation. Actionable 4-tier decision tree and pre-approved response statements.

Problem it addresses

Build an open-source social media crisis management protocol: 1) Four-tier crisis classification matrix (Tier 1: Isolated Complaint, Tier 2: Emerging Trend, Tier 3: Influencer Backlash, Tier 4: Viral PR Outrage), 2) Decision tree mapping when to respond, when to pause ads, and when to escalate to C-suite, 3) Bilingual Arabic/English holding statement swipe files, 4) Post-crisis retrospection template.

Who it is for

BOTH operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Severity Escalation Matrix: Categorizes brand incidents into Level 1 (Minor Grievance), Level 2 (Public Backlash), and Level 3 (Regulatory Threat).
  • Response Time SLA: Mandates initial containment holding statements within 60 minutes for high-severity viral incidents.
  • Cross-Departmental Governance: Coordinates public PR statements with legal, customer support, and executive leadership.
