Short-Form Video 3-Second Thumbstop Hook Benchmark

A bilingual set of illustrative short-form video hook examples, plus an offline retention-curve analyzer. No measured hook-performance benchmark is claimed.

Problem it addresses

Provide a small, expandable set of Arabic and English hook examples organized by category, an offline retention-curve analyzer, and a skill for drafting context-specific hooks. The included examples and retention fixtures are illustrative; they do not establish market performance.

Who it is for

BOTH operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Quick Start from the README: python -m unittest discover -s tests · python src/retention_analyzer.py --help

Scope and limitations

  • 3-Second Thumbstop Metric: A user-supplied campaign metric: calculate it from the selected platform export and compare against that account’s own baseline. This repository provides no universal target or measured benchmark.
  • Visual Pattern Interrupts: Audits presence of rapid visual motion, unexpected text overlays, or sound cues within the first 1.5 seconds.
  • Dialectal Hook Examples: Includes illustrative openings such as 'أكبر غلطة...' and 'لو ساكن في الرياض...'; they are not represented as validated top performers.

This page summarizes the available documentation; it does not establish performance or business results. Verify data and prerequisites before use.
