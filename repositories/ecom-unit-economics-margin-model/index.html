E-Commerce Contribution Margin & Break-Even Unit Economics Model

Know your real profit before scaling ad spend. Calculate exact contribution margins and break-even ROAS factoring GCC payment fees and COD returns.

Problem it addresses

Build an open-source e-commerce unit economics and break-even ROAS calculation toolkit: 1) Single-file responsive HTML/JS calculator (unit_economics.html) calculating Contribution Margin 1, 2, and 3, factoring Product COGS, Shipping, Payment Fees (Mada, Credit Card, Tamara 6.5%), Return Rates, and Ad CAC, 2) Excel financial model with sensitivity tables, 3) Break-even ROAS formula guide.

Who it is for

BOTH operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Fully-Loaded Landed Cost: Incorporates COGS, international freight, customs and tax assumptions, clearance fees, and Saudi VAT inputs that must be verified for the product and shipment.
  • Payment Gateway & COD Surcharges: Models payment and COD fees using editable assumptions supplied by the merchant.
  • Contribution Margin 3 (CM3): Calculates true profitability after deducting marketing acquisition costs (CAC) and estimated return-to-origin losses.

This page summarizes the available documentation; it does not establish performance or business results. Verify data and prerequisites before use.
