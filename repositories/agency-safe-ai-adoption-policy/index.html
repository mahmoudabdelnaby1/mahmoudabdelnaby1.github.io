Marketing Agency Safe AI Adoption & Data Privacy Policy

Deploy AI across your agency safely without client data leaks. Enterprise AI usage policy, RFP disclosure clauses, and automated PII scrubber.

Problem it addresses

Build an open-source Marketing Agency AI Adoption and Privacy Pack: 1) Complete corporate AI usage policy (Approved vs Restricted tools, Model training opt-outs, Copyright guidelines), 2) Client RFP compliance disclosure addendum, 3) Python PII data scrubber script (scrub_pii.py) anonymizing names, phones, and emails before AI prompting, 4) Employee AI onboarding checklist.

Who it is for

PRO operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Client Data Isolation: Prohibits pasting sensitive client PII, financial statements, or unreleased product roadmaps into public AI model prompts.
  • Enterprise Opt-Out Verification: Mandates use of enterprise AI tiers with zero data retention and explicit exclusion from public model training datasets.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Deliverable Accountability: Requires human agency review and fact-checking of every AI-assisted client deliverable before client delivery.
