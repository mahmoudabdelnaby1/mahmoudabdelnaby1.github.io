RFC 8058 One-Click Unsubscribe & Deliverability Sentinel

Verify Google and Yahoo RFC 8058 compliance, test SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, and protect your domain sender reputation.

Problem it addresses

Build an open-source email deliverability and header audit CLI (deliverability_sentinel.py): 1) Parses raw .eml or RFC 822 email files, 2) Validates presence and syntax of List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click, 3) Checks DNS domain alignment for SPF, DKIM, and DMARC policies, 4) Outputs an executive compliance scorecard with pass/fail terminal status.

Who it is for

PRO operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Header-Based One-Click Unsubscribe: Enforces presence of valid 'List-Unsubscribe: <https:...>' and 'List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click' headers.
  • HTTPS POST Processing: Unsubscribe URLs must execute unsubscription upon receipt of an HTTP POST request without redirecting to a confirmation landing page.
  • Bulk Sender Compliance: Aligns with mandatory 2024 Gmail and Yahoo sender guidelines for domains sending >5,000 emails daily.
