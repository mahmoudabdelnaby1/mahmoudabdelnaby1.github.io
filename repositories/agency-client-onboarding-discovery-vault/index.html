Agency Client Onboarding Brand Discovery Vault & Credential SOP

A structured marketing-client onboarding vault with brand discovery prompts and secure platform-access delegation steps. The time required depends on the client and access process.

Problem it addresses

Create an open-source agency client onboarding system: 1) Deep Brand Discovery intake questionnaire (Brand Voice, Target ICP, Unit Economics, Past Campaign Failures, Approved Assets), 2) Visual step-by-step platform access delegation guide (Meta Business Portfolio, Google Ads MCC, GA4, TikTok Business Center, Shopify Staff), 3) 7-day onboarding kick-off milestone schedule.

Who it is for

PRO operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Zero Shared Password Policy: Mandates role-based enterprise access via Meta Business Portfolio, Google MCC, and TikTok Business Center rather than raw logins.
  • Brand Asset Intake Governance: Systematic collection of vector logos, brand books, typography licenses, and past campaign performance data.
  • Stakeholder SLA Alignment: Establishes clear feedback turnaround SLAs and change request protocols during the first 30 days of client engagement.
