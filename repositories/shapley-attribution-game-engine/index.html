Cooperative Game Theory Shapley Multi-Touch Attribution Engine

Distribute marketing credit fairly using Cooperative Game Theory. Compare Shapley attribution against First-Touch and Last-Touch with zero dependencies.

Problem it addresses

Build a standalone Python multi-touch attribution engine (shapley_attribution.py): 1) Implements exact Shapley value calculation from user touchpoint journey strings (e.g., 'Google > Facebook > Email > Conversion'), 2) Compares Shapley credit against First-Touch, Last-Touch, Linear, and Time-Decay models, 3) Exports an interactive HTML comparison table and bar chart, 4) Includes 1,000 synthetic multi-channel touchpoint journeys.

Who it is for

PRO operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Combinatorial Complexity ($2^N$): Computes exact Shapley marginal contributions for up to 10 distinct marketing channels; uses Monte Carlo sampling for larger sets.
  • First-Touch & Last-Touch Bias Elimination: Fairly distributes revenue credit to middle-funnel touchpoints (e.g. email, content) undervalued by default ad platform reports.
  • Path Deduplication: Sequences repeated visits from identical channels within short timeframes (e.g. 1 hour) into single consolidated interactions.
