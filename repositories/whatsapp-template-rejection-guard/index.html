WhatsApp Business Template Rejection Guard & Compliance Linter

Check selected WhatsApp template structure and flag possible policy issues before submission. Meta reviews and approves templates; this linter cannot guarantee acceptance.

Problem it addresses

Build an open-source WhatsApp Business template compliance checker: 1) Python linter (wa_template_linter.py) scanning template text for disallowed promotional trigger words, sequential variable constraints ({{1}}, {{2}}), and required unsubscribe/opt-out phrasing, 2) Four illustrative template examples; they are not pre-tested or Meta-approved (Order Updates, COD Verification, Cart Abandonment, Back-in-Stock), 3) Meta policy cheat sheet.

Who it is for

BOTH operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Variable Parameter Spacing: Flags templates where dynamic variables (e.g. {{1}}) lack surrounding space or contain trailing punctuation that causes Meta API rejections.
  • Category Classification Alignment: Audits template text to ensure Marketing, Utility, and Authentication templates are correctly declared per Meta pricing tiers.
  • Prohibited Content Screening: Screens text against Meta commercial policies prohibiting deceptive promises, sensitive health claims, and gambling terms.
