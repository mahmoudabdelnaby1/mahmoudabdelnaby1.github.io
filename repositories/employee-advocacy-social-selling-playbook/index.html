Employee Advocacy & Social Selling Operational Playbook

Transform your team into trusted brand advocates. Modern employee social media policy and 52-week authentic content prompt kit.

Problem it addresses

Build an open-source corporate employee advocacy framework: 1) Modern, empowering social media employee policy (clear boundaries without stifling creativity), 2) 52-week employee content prompt calendar categorized by department (Engineering, Sales, Customer Support, Leadership), 3) Recognition and gamification program design, 4) Complete 1-hour workshop slide deck outline.

Who it is for

PRO operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.
