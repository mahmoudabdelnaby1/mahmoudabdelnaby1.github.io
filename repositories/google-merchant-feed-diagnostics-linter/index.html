Google Merchant Center Feed Diagnostics & Catalog Integrity Linter

Stop product disapprovals in Google Shopping. Audit XML and CSV product feeds against Google Merchant Center specifications in seconds.

Problem it addresses

Develop a pure Python Google Merchant Center feed linter (gmc_feed_linter.py): 1) Parses XML (RSS 2.0 / Atom) and TSV/CSV product feeds, 2) Validates mandatory attributes (id, title, description, link, image_link, availability, price with ISO currency, brand, gtin), 3) Checks for prohibited title formatting (ALL CAPS, marketing fluff), 4) Outputs an executive terminal error summary and CSV export of defective SKUs.

Who it is for

PRO operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Mandatory XML/TSV Attribute Completeness: Validates presence of id, title, description, link, image_link, availability, price, gtin, and brand.
  • Currency & VAT Inclusivity: Enforces localized ISO 4217 currencies (SAR, AED, EGP) and mandates prices inclusive of statutory VAT for consumer feeds.
  • Google Taxonomy Categorization: Validates that product_type and google_product_category conform to Google's official taxonomy numerical IDs.
