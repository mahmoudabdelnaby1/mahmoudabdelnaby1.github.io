Agency Scope-of-Work (SOW) Change Order Governance Framework

Stop doing free work for demanding clients. Professional Scope of Work boundary matrix and diplomatic Change Order negotiation scripts.

Problem it addresses

Build an open-source agency Scope of Work and Change Order governance kit: 1) Scope boundary matrix clearly separating Core Deliverables vs Out-of-Scope Add-ons across 6 marketing disciplines, 2) Legally binding 1-page Change Order addendum template, 3) Diplomatic email scripts for saying 'Yes, and here is what it will cost', 4) Client expectation-setting onboarding slides.

Who it is for

PRO operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.
