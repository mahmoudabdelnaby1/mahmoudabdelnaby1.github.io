Saudi Mawthooq & Advertising Regulatory Compliance Handbook

Launch ads in Saudi Arabia with zero legal risk. 30-point regulatory compliance handbook covering Mawthooq, VAT, and GAMR ad rules.

Problem it addresses

Create an open-source Saudi advertising regulatory compliance pack: 1) Master handbook synthesizing GAMR, Ministry of Commerce, and ZATCA ad rules, 2) 30-point pre-flight campaign compliance checklist, 3) Mandatory disclosure copy templates (Mawthooq number, VAT breakdown, commercial registration), 4) Restricted categories and prohibited claims guide (medical, cosmetics, financial).

Who it is for

BOTH operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Mawthooq Platform Mandate: All individuals publishing commercial advertising on social platforms in KSA must hold an active Mawthooq permit.
  • Commercial Disclosure Placement: Sponsored posts must explicitly display the statutory ad disclosure badge (#إعلان or #مادة_إعلانية) prominently.
  • Sanctions & Penalties Framework: Outlines legal repercussions for advertising unlicensed financial, medical, or real estate products under GAMR regulations.
