Dialect-Aware Copywriting Adaptation Agent Skill

Stop AI from mixing Arabic dialects. An authoritative Agent Skill that writes native Egyptian, Saudi, and Emirati ad copy with zero vocabulary bleed.

Problem it addresses

Create an authoritative Agent Skill (SKILL.md) for dialect-aware Arabic marketing copy: 1) Strict dialect isolation rules preventing vocabulary bleed between Egyptian, Saudi (Najdi/Hejazi), and Emirati vernacular, 2) Tone matching (Casual UGC vs Premium B2B vs Conversational Direct Response), 3) Reference vocabulary guides, 4) Worked transformation test fixtures.

Who it is for

BOTH operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Morphological Particle Adaptation: Adapts future tense markers (هـ in Egyptian vs بـ / راح in Gulf) and negation particles (مش vs مو) to preserve natural vernacular flow.
  • Cultural Metaphor Relevance: Replaces regionally specific Egyptian cultural references with equivalent Gulf commercial hooks.
  • Demonstrative Editorial Selection: Provides illustrative transformation rules and editorial patterns, not an exhaustive academic dialect atlas.
