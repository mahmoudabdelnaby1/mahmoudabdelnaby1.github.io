Arabic Schema.org JSON-LD Linter & Entity Validator

Validate JSON-LD syntax and flag selected Arabic text or encoding issues. Passing checks cannot guarantee rich results or search eligibility.

Problem it addresses

Build an Arabic-focused Schema.org JSON-LD linter in pure Python. It must validate: 1) UTF-8 encoding and character escaping for Arabic text, 2) Schema.org Organization, LocalBusiness, Product, and FAQPage schemas, 3) Middle East specific business fields (taxID, vatID, addressCountry, telephone E.164). Include 12 pre-tested JSON-LD templates.

Who it is for

BOTH operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Valid Schema.org Types: Validates standard schema vocabularies including Organization, LocalBusiness, Product, and FAQPage.
  • Arabic UTF-8 & Right-to-Left Encoding: Ensures JSON-LD entity names, descriptions, and localized strings are properly formatted in UTF-8 without corrupting Arabic characters.
  • MENA Regional Business Identifiers: Supports regional business fields such as VAT registration (taxID), commercial registration numbers, and E.164 regional phone formats.
