Bilingual Arabic-English Copywriting Cadence & Punchiness Meter

Make your copy impossible to stop reading. Measure sentence rhythm and eliminate bloated filler words in Arabic and English.

Problem it addresses

Build an open-source copywriting rhythm and cadence analyzer in Python (cadence_meter.py): 1) Analyzes sentence word-length variance (calculating cadence score and visual rhythm wave), 2) Detects configured passive or filler phrases in Arabic and English, 3) Suggests direct, punchy alternatives, 4) Outputs an interactive HTML visual report highlighting monotonous paragraphs.

Who it is for

BOTH operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Syllable & Word Length Ratios: Measures reading cadence in Arabic and English, calculating syllables-per-word and sentence punchiness.
  • Arabic Morphological Complexity: Distinguishes between heavy classical agglutinative words and punchy modern colloquial advertising copy.
  • CTA Readability Scoring: Flags wordy, passive CTAs and recommends concise, action-oriented regional verbs.
