Arabic Social Listening Sentiment Classification Rules & Lexicon

Explore a small illustrative Arabic sentiment lexicon and rule-based classifier. It is not a validated 600-rule corpus, and its outputs require human review.

Problem it addresses

Build an open-source dialectal Arabic social listening sentiment package: 1) Illustrative dialectal sentiment lexicon categorized into True Positive, True Negative, Sarcastic/Ironic Negative, and Delivery/Service Complaints across Egyptian and Gulf vernacular, 2) Python heuristic classifier (arabic_sentiment.py), 3) Unit tests for selected classifier behaviors.

Who it is for

PRO operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Sarcasm & Dialectal Inversion: Accounts for common Arabic sarcastic expressions where surface positive words convey negative sentiment.
  • Regional Morphology Handling: Distinguishes between Egyptian (مش حلو), Gulf (مو زين), and Levant (مو منيح) negation structures.
  • Auditable Heuristic Ruleset: Rule definitions run locally using regex and token lookups without requiring black-box paid APIs.
