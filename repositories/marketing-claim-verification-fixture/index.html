Marketing Claim Fact-Checking & Source Verification Fixture

Protect your brand from AI hallucinations and fake statistics. Extract, verify, and audit marketing claims before publishing.

Problem it addresses

Build an open-source marketing claim verification system: 1) Agent Skill (SKILL.md) that extracts factual assertions, percentages, pricing claims, and competitive comparisons from marketing copy, 2) Python CLI script (extract_claims.py) that surfaces unverified numerical claims for human review, 3) 20-point legal and regulatory compliance checklist for consumer claims.

Who it is for

BOTH operating in high-growth e-commerce, digital publishing, and direct-response marketing across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Egypt.

Scope and limitations

  • Auditable Claim Evidence: Replaces vague marketing superlatives with verifiable factual assertions and documented performance metrics.
  • Saudi Statutory Anti-Misleading Rules: Verifies compliance with Saudi Ministry of Commerce prohibitions against false advertising.
  • Testimonial Attribution Standards: Mandates documented user consent and proof of real customer status before publishing testimonials.
