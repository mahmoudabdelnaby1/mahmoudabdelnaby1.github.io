# LinkedIn Post Editorial Review

Improve clarity, usefulness, evidence, and readability without claiming access to ranking weights.

## When to use it
Use the review before publishing a LinkedIn post or adapting a longer idea into a concise professional update.

## What you need
Audience; one reader takeaway; source material; author voice; call to action; disclosure requirements.

## Workflow
1. State the reader's question and the post's single point.
2. Replace unsupported generalities with an example or source.
3. Tighten the opening and paragraph flow without manufacturing outrage.
4. Check factual accuracy, attribution, privacy, and any commercial disclosure.
5. Preview on mobile and confirm the CTA matches the intended discussion.

## Practical output
An edited post plus a short note explaining the substantive changes.

## Scope and limits
LinkedIn does not publish complete ranking weights. Treat performance observations as hypotheses and assess them with your own data.
