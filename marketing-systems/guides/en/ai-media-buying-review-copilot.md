# AI Media Buying Review Copilot

Structure campaign diagnosis and recommendations while keeping account changes under human control.

## When to use it
Use the prompt with campaign exports when you need a documented diagnosis, prioritized tests, or a second review of a proposed change.

## What you need
Campaign objective; date range; spend and outcome data; tracking notes; changes made; business constraints.

## Workflow
1. Check data completeness and reporting definitions before interpreting performance.
2. Ask for missing context rather than infer a cause from one metric.
3. Rank hypotheses by evidence, impact, and reversibility.
4. Propose a test with a baseline, success measure, and stop condition.
5. Require a named human approver before any account write or publication.

## Practical output
A diagnosis with evidence, confidence, next test, and approval requirement.

## Scope and limits
An AI recommendation is not account access, platform truth, or a performance guarantee. Verify data and current platform guidance.
