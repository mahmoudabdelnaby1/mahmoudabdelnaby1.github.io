# Education Lead Quality Review

Connect lead capture to outcomes admissions teams can verify: contactability, agreed qualification, appointments, and enrollment.

## When to use it
Lead volume looks healthy, but the team cannot tell which inquiries are reachable, fit a program, progress to an appointment, or enroll.

## What you need
Program facts and entry requirements; form and privacy notice; CRM stages and owners; a dated qualification definition; campaign IDs; current platform documentation.

## Workflow
1. Define submitted, contactable, valid, qualified, appointment, application, and enrolled as separate stages with a source of truth.
2. Review form clarity, field necessity, market/language fit, consent purpose, validation, and confirmation.
3. Trace a controlled test record into the CRM; inspect identifiers, duplicates, routing, required fields, and failure handling.
4. Document the business-owned response window, queue owner, opt-out/suppression, and escalation based on actual team capacity.
5. Map each stage to measurement. Verify consent, data minimization, event meaning, and current platform requirements before sharing any signal back.
6. Compare platform-reported leads with CRM-qualified and enrollment records using a declared period, denominator, and attribution limits.
7. Choose one next test, retain a baseline, and define a review or stop condition.

## Practical output
A form review, qualification rule, lead-stage map, CRM handoff checklist, measurement map, and decision memo with evidence, gaps, owners, and a next test.

## Scope and limits
This is not a Salla connector, CRM integration, compliance certification, or performance promise. Avoid unnecessary sensitive or minor data. Event receipt does not prove lead quality, attribution, or enrollment.
