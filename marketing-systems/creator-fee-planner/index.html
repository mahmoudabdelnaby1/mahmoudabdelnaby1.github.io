How the estimate is calculated

Creator payout = fixed fee + other entered costs + capped bonus. The bonus equals eligible views above the threshold ÷ 1,000 × the entered rate, up to the entered cap. Campaign total is the per-creator payout multiplied by creator count.

This is a planning calculator, not a rate benchmark, contract, legal or regulatory check, view verification, or guarantee of reach, engagement, or sales. Confirm deliverables, usage rights, disclosure, payment terms, and any local requirements with the relevant parties before contracting.
