Ad Account Restriction Review & Response

Organize evidence and next steps after an account or asset restriction without promising prevention or reinstatement.

When to use it

Use this framework to document what changed, preserve evidence, and prepare an accurate platform review request.

What you need

Exact platform notice; affected asset IDs; account history; recent edits; policy text cited by the platform; supporting records.

Workflow

  1. Capture the notice, timestamp, affected assets, and current account state.
  2. Pause risky edits; preserve relevant records and campaign context.
  3. Compare the cited policy with the actual ad, destination, and account behavior.
  4. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions and identify a corrective action if needed.
  5. Submit a concise, accurate review request through the platform's official channel and track the response.

Practical output

An incident record, evidence checklist, and factual review-request draft.

Scope and limits

No checklist can make an account ban-proof or guarantee reinstatement. Follow official policy and appeal routes; do not disguise the underlying offer.
